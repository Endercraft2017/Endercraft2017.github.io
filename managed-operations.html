Managed Operations

When work still needs people, AFK³ runs the process with you.

Administrative operations, customer operations, technical support and specialized remote talent — supported by the same systems and reporting AFK³ builds for clients.

What this covers

Operational support, run properly.

Administrative Operations

Recurring administrative work handled to a consistent standard, with clear reporting on what's been done.

Customer Operations

Inquiries, orders and support requests handled by people who know your process, not a generic script.

Technical Support

Ongoing technical support for the systems AFK³ builds, or the ones you already run.

Specialized Remote Talent

Dedicated remote personnel matched to the specific skill your operation needs.

Managed Processes

A defined process run end-to-end on your behalf, with the same systems and reporting AFK³ builds for clients.

How an engagement runs

From scope to a running process.

  1. 01

    Scope

    Define exactly which process AFK³ will run, the standard expected, and how it's measured.

  2. 02

    Onboard

    Set up access, systems and reporting, and get the right people assigned to your process.

  3. 03

    Run

    AFK³ operates the process day to day, with the same tools and visibility you'd expect from an internal team.

  4. 04

    Report & Improve

    Regular reporting on what's been done, plus ongoing adjustments as the process or volume changes.
