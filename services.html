Common questions

What's the difference between custom software, automation, and managed operations?

Custom software means practical systems built around how your team actually works. Automation & AI connects the systems you already run and lets repetitive steps move on their own. Managed operations means AFK³ runs the process with you when work still needs people, supported by the same systems and reporting.

Do I need custom software or just automation?

Can I start smaller before a full build?

Yes. Many engagements start with an MVP — a lower initial commitment that validates the core workflow and gets real user feedback early, before expanding to a fuller build.
