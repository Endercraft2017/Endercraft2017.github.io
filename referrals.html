Referral partners

Know a business
we could help?

Introduce a suitable client to AFK³. We handle discovery, scoping, delivery and collections. This is an optional referral arrangement with no salary, required shift or guaranteed earnings.

A relevant introduction comes first.

Who makes a useful partner?

Independent marketers, consultants and other professionals who already understand a business’s needs and can make an introduction with permission.

Who should you introduce?

A decision-maker with a specific manual workflow, software requirement or interest in reviewing OTOM. Confirm the need, timing and willingness to discuss a budget.

Please do not send a client list or private client records in your first inquiry.

Proposed commission range

10–25%

One exact rate and eligible sale amount are agreed in writing before an introduction is accepted. The rate depends on the agreed contribution, deal economics and scope. Not every sale qualifies for 25%.

This page opens a discussion. It is not a signed partner agreement or a job offer.

Paid as the client’s payments clear.

  1. 01

    Agree on terms

    Confirm the rate, eligible contract, attribution window, exclusions and payout dates before the introduction.

  2. 02

    Make a permitted introduction

    We confirm lead ownership and qualify the opportunity. Partners cannot quote prices or make commitments for AFK³.

  3. 03

    Receive installment payments

    Commission follows eligible cleared client collections. Refunds and chargebacks are handled under the written agreement.

An example, not a price offer

For an agreed ₱40,000 eligible sale at 15%, total commission is ₱6,000. If the client pays ₱20,000, ₱10,000 and ₱10,000, the corresponding commission is ₱3,000, ₱1,500 and ₱1,500 after those funds clear.

The written agreement defines taxes, pass-through costs, refunds, duplicate leads and any recurring commission. A payment screenshot or held balance does not establish cleared funds.
