Before you book

Questions about an AI-built app review

What does AFK³ review?

AFK³ Solutions reviews a defined workflow in an existing AI-built web app. Diagnosis follows the task through its interface, backend and relevant integrations, records reproducible findings, and recommends the next step. Platform and repository access must be confirmed first.

How much does diagnosis cost?

Diagnosis is paid. We agree on the workflow, investigation budget and deliverables before starting. There is no universal price listed here: access, reproducibility and scope determine the quote. Repair is priced separately after diagnosis.

Should I repair or rebuild?

That depends on the cause, platform constraints and remaining work. Diagnosis can recommend a focused repair, a replacement component or stopping investment. It does not assume that a complete rebuild is necessary.

Can I use another developer afterward?

Yes. The diagnosis includes findings, evidence, priorities and a repair recommendation you can take to another developer. You do not have to buy a repair sprint or ongoing support.

Do you need private customer data?

Start with screenshots or a recording without private records. Use demonstration data and owner-authorized access in an isolated environment for investigation. Do not send credentials or customer records through the inquiry form.

Does a review certify the app for launch?

No. Findings apply to the agreed scope and tested environment. Security certification, compliance sign-off and a guaranteed launch date are not part of this review. Acceptance checks and deployment responsibilities are agreed explicitly.

Our own engineering example

A source tag could interrupt an inquiry.

This is an internal AFK³ website repair, not a customer case or a measured sales result.

In a controlled local reproduction, utm_source=chatgpt was accepted, but utm_source=chatgpt.com caused our tracking serializer to throw an error. The form could stop before submitting the inquiry.

The change and its verification

Read the full reproduction and repair walkthrough →

The patch accepts bounded domain-style campaign values in the browser and server. Malformed optional tags are omitted instead of blocking an otherwise valid inquiry. Referral codes retain their existing format; referral credit is still confirmed separately.

Automated checks cover source preservation, the inquiry form, the server handler, and a no-JavaScript fallback. Email delivery is mocked in these tests. They do not prove production inbox delivery, AI citations or increased sales.

Bring the app and the problem.

In your first inquiry, tell us what the app does, its platform or stack, the step that breaks, its business impact, your intended deadline and approximate investigation budget. Screenshots or a recording can help.

We confirm relevant experience and access before quoting. A review may recommend a small repair, a platform feature, a replacement component or stopping investment in an unsuitable app.

Access and boundaries

Do not send passwords, API keys or private customer records through the inquiry form. After scope is agreed, arrange owner-authorized repository access and an isolated test environment with suitable demonstration data.

Production changes require an agreed deployment and rollback plan. Specialist penetration testing, security certification, compliance sign-off and guaranteed launch dates are not included in this engineering review.
