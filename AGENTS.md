# AFK³ website review rule

For every change involving the website, a webapp presentation, or a service, answer:
"Is our website, webapp, or service referenceable?"

Judge this from crawlable public information, a clear buyer and offer, claims supported
by observed behavior or linked evidence, accurate readiness and limitations, and a
working next step that preserves attribution. Report each unresolved gap. Never infer
indexing, AI citations, customer validation, or commercial results from local tests.
Keep private credentials and customer data out of evidence. Preserve training opt-outs
when enabling search crawlers. Prefer genuine proof over more pages or AI-only files.
