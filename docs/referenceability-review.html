What counts as a result

Record a brand mention, an actual source citation and a vendor recommendation separately. Flag unsupported price, readiness, security or customer claims. A supplied-URL summary is a retrieval/comprehension check, not discovery. Use Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools for account evidence when access is available. Inspect real AI referrals through the inquiry payload; qualify leads and track attended calls, paid diagnoses and repairs separately.

At low volume, publish counts and examples. A few citations or one lead do not establish causation or a statistically proven improvement. We can improve eligibility and useful evidence; engines decide whether to use it.
