## What the comparisons do and do not establish

The observed marketplace pages give search an explicit service match and give buyers comparable scope, price, timing and a named provider. We cannot infer which of those fields caused an AI recommendation.

One off-site Kayvandra query returned no results, despite its earlier recommendation. External mentions are therefore not established as a prerequisite by this audit. DevDec has directory and company-post coverage in returned results, but that does not demonstrate those pages caused its recommendation. FixedLaunch's off-site results included self-authored or mirrored material; those are not independent endorsements.

The evidence supports testing distribution through a marketplace. It does not support promising that copying a competitor's price, joining a directory or publishing more pages will produce AI recommendations.

## Proposed marketplace pilot, draft only

Publication status: not created, submitted or published in Upwork. No account has been selected or authenticated. No message was sent and no purchase or paid promotion was made.

### Listing title

Diagnose one broken workflow in your AI-built web app

### Draft customer-facing summary

I diagnose one agreed, reproducible workflow failure in an existing web application, such as a form that reports success without saving, an API integration that fails, or a deployment-related error.

The diagnosis follows the task through the relevant interface, application code and connected services. You receive the agreed workflow and expected behavior, reproduction steps, observed findings, the confirmed cause where established, and a prioritized repair recommendation with proposed acceptance checks.

Diagnosis does not include implementing every fix. Repairs are quoted separately, and the findings can be used by another developer. If access or an external dependency prevents a conclusion, the report identifies that limit and the evidence still needed.

Please describe what should happen, what actually happens and the application's stack before ordering, so we can confirm fit. Use demonstration data and access you can revoke. Do not send passwords, secret keys or private customer records.

### Proposed deliverables

- One workflow and tested environment recorded.
- Reproduction steps and supporting observations.
- Cause where established, with unresolved questions explicitly separated.
- Repair recommendation, acceptance checks and remaining limits.
- No blanket security certification or guaranteed launch.

### Commercial fields still required

The existing proposal of US$99 for up to two engineering hours remains unapproved. Price, delivery setting, revision policy and capacity must be settled before submission. The website remains quotation-based. Do not copy the earlier business-day proposal into an Upwork delivery field without checking how that field counts days and confirming availability.

Upwork states that Project Catalog requires price, scope, delivery days, requirements and work samples. It reviews submissions, and approval may take weeks for freelancers outside its specified talent tiers. Service fees apply to catalog work; the fee shown for the actual contract must be included in the economics before accepting work.
