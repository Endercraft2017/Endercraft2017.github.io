# Proposed diagnosis offer for owner review

Status: draft, not published or an accepted customer commitment. Prepared 2 November 2026.

## Recommended launch offer

**AI App Workflow Diagnosis: US$99 for one workflow, up to two engineering hours.**

For a founder or small agency with an existing AI-built web app and a clearly described broken task. We confirm stack fit, authorized access and a suitable demonstration environment before accepting payment.

Proposed delivery window: within three business days of complete access and scope agreement, subject to confirming capacity before acceptance.

Deliverables:
- Agreed workflow, expected behavior and reproduction steps.
- Short screen recording or annotated screenshots of observed behavior where reproducible.
- Confirmed cause when established; hypotheses and unknowns labelled separately.
- Prioritized repair recommendation and proposed acceptance checks.
- A separately scoped repair quotation when enough evidence exists to price one.

The fee purchases a bounded investigation and findings, not a guaranteed repair or diagnosis of every problem. Two hours is the investigation cap, not a promise that every cause can be established in that time. Additional work requires a new agreement. Diagnosis can recommend repair, component replacement, further investigation or stopping investment. Findings can be used with another developer.

No automatic subscription or repair-fee credit. No production changes during diagnosis. No security certification, compliance sign-off, penetration test, active-incident response or launch guarantee. Hosting and third-party charges are excluded. Existing terms and payment/refund arrangements need to be confirmed in the written engagement before payment.

## Before publishing

Owner must approve the price, two-hour investigation cap and delivery capacity. Do not replace quotation-based public pricing until approved. Use the current service page for listing requests while this is pending.
