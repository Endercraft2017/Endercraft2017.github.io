# Privacy Policy

## 2. Introduction

AFK³ Solutions ("AFK³", "we", "us", or "our") respects the privacy of
individuals who visit our website, contact us, submit business
inquiries, communicate with us, or otherwise interact with AFK³.

This Privacy Policy explains how AFK³ may collect, use, store, disclose,
and otherwise process personal information in connection with:

-   The AFK³ website;
-   Project and service inquiries;
-   Business communications;
-   Prospective client relationships;
-   Client administration;
-   Supplier, contractor, and business-partner relationships; and
-   Other direct interactions with AFK³.

AFK³ currently operates from the Philippines. Personal information may
therefore be processed subject to the Republic Act No. 10173, also known
as the **Data Privacy Act of 2012**, its implementing rules and
regulations, and other applicable privacy requirements.

Additional privacy or data-protection requirements may apply depending
on the location of the relevant individual, client, service provider, or
processing activity.

This Privacy Policy should be read together with the AFK³ Terms &
Conditions and any applicable project-specific agreement.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3. Scope of This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy primarily describes how AFK³ processes personal
information for its own website, business-development, administrative,
and direct business purposes.

AFK³ may also process information **on behalf of clients** when
developing, maintaining, integrating, automating, or operating client
systems.

Where AFK³ processes personal information on behalf of a client, the
client's own privacy policy, instructions, project agreement, Data
Processing Agreement ("DPA"), or other applicable contractual
arrangement may govern that processing.

In those circumstances, the client may act as the party primarily
determining why and how the relevant personal information is processed,
while AFK³ processes the information in accordance with the applicable
engagement.

This public Privacy Policy is not intended to replace project-specific
privacy, security, confidentiality, or data-processing agreements where
those are required.

This is a legal/privacy page.

Avoid:

-   Background video;
-   Large mascot animation;
-   Scroll hijacking;
-   Strong parallax;
-   Excessive particles;
-   Distracting glowing effects.

Acceptable:

-   Subtle section reveal;
-   Small cyan dividers;
-   Smooth anchor navigation;
-   Subtle active-section indicator.

Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
