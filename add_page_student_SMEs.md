# AFK³ Solutions — New Audience Landing Pages

## Objective

Add TWO new landing pages to the existing AFK³ Solutions website:

1. Small Business / SME Landing Page
2. Student Landing Page

These are NEW landing pages.

DO NOT redesign, replace, or significantly modify any existing AFK³ pages.

In particular, do NOT rewrite:

- Homepage
- Services
- How We Work
- Work
- About
- Existing business contact page

The existing website is the source of truth for AFK³'s:

- Branding
- Colors
- Typography
- Header
- Footer
- Buttons
- Components
- Animations
- Spacing
- Responsive behavior
- Overall visual language

Before building the new pages, inspect the existing codebase and reuse
existing components/styles wherever appropriate.

The new pages should look like they have always belonged to the existing
AFK³ website.


# PURPOSE OF THE LANDING PAGES

These pages are targeted entry points for specific audiences coming from:

- Facebook
- Social media posts
- Direct links
- Outreach
- Marketing campaigns

They should be more focused and conversion-oriented than the general
AFK³ Services page.

The user should immediately recognize:

"This is for someone like me."

Each landing page should have ONE clear audience and ONE primary
conversion objective.

## TARGET AUDIENCE

Small and growing businesses that:

- Still rely heavily on spreadsheets
- Perform repetitive administrative work
- Manually transfer information between systems
- Handle customer inquiries manually
- Have disconnected tools
- Have inefficient internal processes
- Need better tracking or reporting
- Need custom software
- Want to automate repetitive operations
- Have outgrown their current workflow
- Need technology but do not have a large internal development team


## PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Get the business owner to:

DISCUSS THEIR BUSINESS PROBLEM WITH AFK³.


## CORE POSITIONING

Do NOT make this page primarily about programming.

The visitor does not need to care whether AFK³ uses Python, React,
FastAPI, APIs, AI models, etc.

Start with the BUSINESS PROBLEM.

Then show how technology can solve it.
